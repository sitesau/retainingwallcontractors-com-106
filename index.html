Preparing a Consistent Brief for Accurate Quotes

Preparing a Consistent Brief for Accurate Quotes

The most common mistake is sending different descriptions to each contractor, which makes price comparisons meaningless. Start by deciding which category best fits the job‑repair, new work, or a specific wall type. Write a short brief that includes the wall material, size, site conditions, and any photos that show the problem area. Use the same brief for every enquiry, whether you call, email, or fill out an online form. The directory's comparison tools work best when you feed them identical information, so the review, photo, and pricing data reflect true differences in scope rather than guesswork. Keep the brief concise‑about 30 seconds of description is enough for contractors to respond with a realistic quote.

If you are unsure about the exact wall type, the category descriptions in the directory can guide your initial choice.
